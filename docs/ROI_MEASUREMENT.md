# ROI measurement

Fortify reports descriptive workflow economics, not predicted premium savings, loss reduction, resilience effectiveness, renewal probability, or causality.

Before use, a customer confirms a baseline window and measurement method for staff minutes by activity, elapsed evidence cycle, clarification/rework events, duplicate collection, external fees, programme contribution decisions, and submission/outcome completeness. Each follow-up report carries cohort, denominator, observation window, missingness, source versions, query receipt, data-right classification, suppression policy, and human review.

Permitted calculations include observed staff-hours difference at the same customer, cycle-time distribution, rework count, documented cost/commitment totals, and report completeness. Separate workflow measures from funding, insurance, rating, underwriting, placement, model, verified physical condition, and claims outcomes. Never convert a correlation into attribution. Cross-customer benchmarks remain prohibited until contractual rights, de-identification, minimum cohort, suppression, and privacy review are all explicit.
