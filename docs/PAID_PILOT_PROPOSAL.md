# Paid design-partner pilot proposal

## Hypothesis

A specialist community-association brokerage will pay for faster, more traceable renewal-evidence workflow independently of any premium outcome.

## Scope

- 10-30 redacted live renewal cases over one renewal cycle.
- One practice leader, 2-5 renewal/account executives, participating community managers, and 1-3 willing underwriter reviewers.
- Configure carrier request templates and selected reference content from partner-provided, permitted source material.
- Organize notices, evidence, tasks, packet generation, clarification, outcome capture, maintenance, and year-over-year reuse.
- Weekly workflow review and a final measured readout.

Excluded: risk scoring, inspections, legal/actuarial certification, official designation, carrier API integration, premium forecasting, marketplace services, and any guarantee of acceptance, renewal, or pricing change.

## Success measures

- Baseline and endline median hours from notice to reviewable packet.
- Percentage of cases with missing, stale, ambiguous-scope, contradictory, or unreviewed evidence at first assembly.
- Deadline adherence and clarification-loop count.
- Underwriter packet open/use rate and structured-feedback rate.
- Prior-year evidence reuse rate with successful revalidation.
- Weekly active renewal executives and cases completed without off-platform reconstruction.
- Renewal executive and underwriter trust rubric with no critical failure.

## Commercial hypothesis

A fixed pilot fee of **$15,000-$30,000** for one renewal cycle is a pricing hypothesis, not established market pricing. It covers configuration, secure onboarding, support, measurement, and the 10-30 case cohort. The partner is not buying premium savings; the value proposition is workflow time, decision traceability, and a reusable evidence record.

Production pricing, if warranted, would be tested separately against case volume, active portfolios, and service level. No free pilot should be used to mask weak willingness to pay.

## Partner commitments

- Provide 10-30 lawfully shareable, redacted live cases and permitted source documents.
- Name an executive sponsor and one workflow owner.
- Recruit willing underwriter reviewers; Fortify will not claim carrier endorsement.
- Participate in baseline timing, weekly review, and endline measurement.
- Approve a data-handling plan before any live information enters a pilot environment.

## Stop conditions

Pause or end the pilot if evidence cannot be handled lawfully, underwriters consistently refuse the packet format, daily users reconstruct cases outside the system, workflow time does not improve, critical provenance errors occur, or the brokerage will not pay independent of premium outcome.
