# Broker and underwriter discovery guide

## Objective

Determine whether renewal-evidence workflow pain is frequent, expensive, and important enough to buy independently of premium savings. Do not sell before understanding the current operating system.

## Practice-leader interview (45 minutes)

1. How many community-association, condominium, townhome, and low-rise multifamily master-policy renewals do you handle each year? How many involve wildfire evidence, nonrenewal, classification dispute, or appeal?
2. Walk through the last difficult case from notice receipt to carrier decision. Which people and systems touched it?
3. How many hours did evidence collection, validation, packet assembly, follow-up, and audit reconstruction take? Which role did each hour?
4. How do carrier requests vary? Which requests are stable enough to template and which require interpretation?
5. In a typical case, what proportion of evidence is missing, stale, ambiguously scoped, contradictory, or unreviewed?
6. How often are prior-year photos, certificates, invoices, or reports safely reusable? What prevents reuse?
7. Which deadlines are most likely to be missed? How are acknowledgement and decision dates tracked today?
8. What must an underwriter see to trust a broker-assembled packet? Would your carrier partners use a structured packet and read-only review link?
9. What must remain human-controlled? What extraction or automation would create unacceptable error?
10. Would you provide 10-30 redacted live cases for one renewal-cycle design-partner pilot? Who could authorize data sharing?
11. What security, retention, identity, and audit requirements would be non-negotiable?
12. Would you pay for evidence workflow and time reduction even if no premium changed? What budget and approval path would apply?

## Daily-user interview (30 minutes)

- Show the actual inbox, spreadsheet, drive tree, and packet from a recent case.
- Ask the user to mark where they decide “good enough to send.”
- Measure handoffs, duplicate entry, evidence-name ambiguity, and rework loops.
- Ask which five fields they must see without opening a file.
- Ask what makes a generated packet embarrassing or untrustworthy.

## Underwriter interview (30 minutes)

- Would you open this packet? Which page first?
- Is the requirement-to-evidence matrix useful or burdensome?
- Which provenance fields are decision-relevant?
- Is structured clarification preferable to email? Why or why not?
- What would make you reject the format regardless of evidence quality?

## Evidence to capture

Record annual case volume, appeals, median and high-case assembly hours, missing/stale/conflicting rates, packet-use willingness, live-case commitment, independent willingness to pay, and a verbatim description of the buying process. Do not treat enthusiasm as validation.
