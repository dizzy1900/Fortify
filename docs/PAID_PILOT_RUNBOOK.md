# Paid pilot runbook

The pilot begins only after security, data-processing, source-rights, deployment, restore, support, and acceptance owners approve their gates. Use one brokerage, one rights-cleared California portfolio, and at least ten redacted/live cases. Fixture evidence never counts.

Before kickoff record contracted scope, roles, data classes/rights, retention/deletion, approved source policy, target profiles, independent verifier, recipient authority, support/escalation, baseline workflow measures, and explicit non-promises. Train users on human confirmations and unavailable/insufficient states.

Weekly measure onboarding progress, import exceptions, evidence cycle time, clarification/rework, staff time, submission delivery custody, response taxonomy completeness, maintenance obligations, incidents, and customer feedback. Separately record real programme, verifier, model-provider, insurer/MGA, and funding decisions without inferring causality.

Exit requires ten completed cases, customer review, security/operations review, actual responses captured, pilot metric report, open-risk register, and an explicit paid-continuation decision. A no-go or pause is a valid result.
