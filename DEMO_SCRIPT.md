# Fortify five-minute demo script

## Setup (before the call)

Run `npm run demo:reset && npm run dev`, open `/demo`, choose **Renewal executive**, and confirm the guide shows step 1 of 9. State that every company, policy, notice, premium, and outcome is fictional.

## 0:00-0:35 - Find the case in danger

On **Portfolio**, point to Fictional Red Rock Townhomes: 140 units, Jefferson County, an appeal deadline in 11 days, stale vegetation evidence, and an invoice-scope conflict. Say: “This is evidence readiness, never predicted wildfire risk or eligibility.”

## 0:35-1:10 - Confirm what the carrier said

Open **Carrier notice**. Show the plain-text source, deterministic parser, seven extracted fields, confidence, and editable values. Change a field if useful, then click **Confirm all fields**. Explain that extraction does not become authoritative until a human confirms it.

## 1:10-1:45 - See the crosswalk

Open **Requirement crosswalk**. Show the six independently visible readiness components, source/version/verify-current fields, and ready/partial/missing/conflict states. Emphasize the selected, non-exhaustive demo summaries and the legal/standards disclaimer.

## 1:45-2:20 - Assign missing evidence work

Open **Renewal case**. Add the vegetation and itemized-invoice tasks. Toggle one complete. Show owners, deadlines, and appended audit history.

## 2:20-2:50 - Disposition a contradiction

Open **Evidence room**, filter to invoices, open the **Provenance** drawer, and click **Resolve conflict** on one conflicting record. Show filename, scope, dates, source, verifier, full hash, expiry, requirement links, review, and carrier-specific status. Explain that the other version is preserved rather than destroyed.

## 2:50-3:35 - Generate the actual packet

Open **Packet builder**, edit one sentence in the reconsideration letter, save, click **Confirm packet contents**, then click **Generate PDF + ZIP**. Download both. Explain that the six-page packet includes the mitigation-action register, and the ZIP contains `manifest.json`, the editable letter, the PDF, and 14 real fixture exhibits with matching MIME metadata and hashes.

## 3:35-4:15 - Review as the underwriter

Switch the role control to **Underwriter**, open **Underwriter review**, and click **Send clarification request**. Point out that the view is read-only: the reviewer cannot alter broker evidence or readiness.

## 4:15-4:40 - Respond and record the fictional outcome

Open **Outcome**, return to the broker role, review the clarification response, and record the clearly fictional classification/renewal/premium outcome. Say explicitly that acceptance and pricing changes are never guaranteed.

## 4:40-5:00 - Show the compounding record

Open **Maintenance**. Show due/scheduled refreshes and the Horsetooth Flats year-over-year reuse example. Close with: “The durable community record is created as a by-product of doing this year’s renewal work well.”

## Recovery

The guide can advance, go back, exit, or resume without covering the product. **Settings → Reset demo** or `npm run demo:reset` restores the exact starting state.
